The Details

Time: People may start arriving at 3:00pm. I am planning on having food ready by 4:30pm. See the Activities page for fun things to do while you wait.

Weather: MeatFest '026 will happen rain or shine. I will have plenty of covered space in the event of rain. That same cover will also be available for shade if it is bright and sunny.

The Cookers!

The Pit Boss 850 Wood Pellet Grill & Smoker will be a headline cooker for MeatFest '026. This will be handeling the brisket.

Vertie is a vertical smoker that will be doing lot of heavy lifting for MeatFest '026. This will be handeling among other things, pork buts, ribs, other smoked meats.

The Halo is a flat top griddle with a warming rack. Its a multi-purpose cooker that will be used for a variety of things.

The Explorer has 2 30,000 BTU burners and will carry the load of the deep fried food deliciousness for MeatFest '026.

The Grid is a 960 sq in (6193.5 sq cm) custom built charcoal grill with a height adjustable rotisserie spit capable of turning a 30 lb (13.63 kg) unbalanced load and a 50 lb (22.72 kg) well balanced load. This will be turning some whole chickens for MeatFest '026. It could also turn a meat creation of your own!
