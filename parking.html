The Details

Location: 4631 Olson Dr  Eau Claire, Wisconsin

Date: Saturday October 3rd 2026

Time: People may start arriving at 3:00pm. I am planning on having food ready by 4:30pm. See the Activities page for fun things to do while you wait.

If you are interested in comming earlier, check out the Participate page for details.

Weather: MeatFest '026 will happen rain or shine. I will have plenty of covered space in the event of rain. That same cover will also be available for shade if it is bright and sunny.

Parking

Onsite parking is available. There should be plenty available for all, but if you can consolidate on vehicles, please do.

Vehicles will enter the ditch and travel along the fence. Round the corner and proceed to the roundabout. At the roundabout, turn left and come around, yielding to any vehicles that may be approaching the roundabout. Proceed back down the path and pull into a parking stall. The path is wide enough to accommodate one-way traffic at a time, and there are several spots along the way for vehicles to pull aside and let another pass if needed. Please pull far enough into your stall so that no part of your vehicle extends past the white line marking the path. Please follow any posted signage. On the map below, the gray boxes indicate parking spaces. If you have concerns about your vehicle getting to a spot; or if you have mobility concerns, please let me know with the contact form so we can make arrangements.
